About and privacy

Without an account, everything stays in this browser on this device. If you sign in, your sessions are also saved to a database in London (Supabase), locked so only your account can read them. GPS is only used while a live session is recording. Route maps hide the first and last 200 m so they don't show where you start or finish. Uploading to Strava sends the full session to Strava, where Strava's own privacy settings apply. There are no ads, cookies or analytics. Deleting your account removes all your sessions from the database.
